About Bluepeak

From bare lands to masterpieces

Bluepeak Real Estate Limited (RC: 8588237) is a Nigerian real estate development and marketing company building residential estates across Abuja.

Who We Are

A developer working district by district

Bluepeak Real Estate Limited markets and develops residential land and housing across multiple Abuja districts — including Apo, Asokoro, Karsana, Ketti/Kabusa, Pyakasa, Katamkpe Extension and Katampe Extension. Estates are brought to market in phases, each carrying its own facility list, pricing and approval status.

Several estates are marketed as FCDA-approved or carrying a Certificate of Occupancy (C of O); prospective buyers should always confirm current documentation status for the specific plot or unit they are considering, directly with our sales office.

Mission & Vision

What we're building toward

Mission

Deliver documented, serviced estates

To bring well-located residential land and housing to market with clear pricing, facility lists and title documentation, so buyers know exactly what they are purchasing.

Vision

A trusted name across Abuja's growth corridors

To be a dependable developer as Abuja's residential frontier extends into districts like Apo, Karsana, Kabusa and Katampe.

Values

Transparency in pricing and documentation

Publishing plot sizes, unit types and current pricing on every estate flyer, and updating buyers directly when prices change.

Approach

Phased development, phased pricing

Each estate is developed and priced in phases — reflected in the promotional pricing and price-increment notices issued as phases sell down.
