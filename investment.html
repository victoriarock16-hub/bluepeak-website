Investment

Strategic locations. Staged pricing.

Property in Abuja's expanding districts is priced in phases — entering earlier can mean entering before a documented price increment.

Why Invest in Abuja

Growth is moving outward from the centre

Districts such as Apo, Karsana, Kabusa, Pyakasa, Katamkpe and Katampe Extension continue to see new residential development as the city expands. We don't publish return projections we can't stand behind — what we can share is what's documented on our own estate materials.

Documented price increments

Dominion Villa's per-unit prices, for example, roughly doubled between the initial listing and the 10 August 2026 increment — a real, dated example of phase-based repricing rather than a projection.

Outright vs. installment terms

Most estates offer a lower outright price and a higher structured installment price — the spread is published on every flyer, not hidden until enquiry.

A spread of entry points

From roughly ₦4.25M land-stage pricing to ₦100M+ fully detached duplexes, across nine estates in six districts.
