FAQ

Common questions, answered plainly

If something below doesn't cover your question, reach us directly through the Contact page.

Bluepeak's current portfolio spans nine estates: Solar City (Apo), Unicorn Garden Estate (Asokoro), Glory Town Estate (Karsana), Dominion Villa (Ketti/Kabusa), Avalon City (Pyakasa), Hill-View Estate Phase 2 (Apo Hilltop), Horizon Villa (Katamkpe Extension), Bluepeak Homes Villa (Apo Tyafi) and Hill Crest (Katampe Extension). Full details for each are on the Properties page.

Approval status is stated per estate where it appears on our official materials — several list an FCDA approval or Certificate of Occupancy (C of O). Where a page doesn't state this, we haven't assumed it; please confirm directly with our office before purchase.

The outright price is the lump-sum figure; the installment price reflects a structured payment plan over time and is typically higher than the outright figure. Both are published side by side on each estate's property page.

A price increment took effect on 10 August 2026, documented on Bluepeak's own materials. We show both the prior and current per-unit prices on the Dominion Villa page, alongside a note about an earlier, separate 1-hectare land promotion that has since lapsed.

Where a facility list is published for an estate, it's shown in full on that estate's page. Facility inclusions vary by estate and by phase — always confirm current inclusions before signing.

Use the Book an Inspection form on the Contact page, or the same-named button in the navigation menu. Choose the estate you'd like to visit and a preferred date, and our team will confirm.

Most estates list an outright price and an installment price; specific installment structures (deposit percentage, number of instalments) are confirmed with our sales team at the point of enquiry, as they aren't uniformly stated across all flyers.
