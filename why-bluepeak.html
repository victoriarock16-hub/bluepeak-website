Why Bluepeak

Nine estates. One published standard.

Every Bluepeak flyer states its location, sizes, pricing and facilities up front — here's what that looks like across the portfolio.

01

Strategic locations

Every estate is anchored to a named district and, in most cases, a neighbouring landmark — beside NAF Valley Estate, sharing a boundary with Renewed Hope Estate, beside ECOWAS Estate, and so on.

02

Quality development

Architectural renders accompany each estate listing, showing the specific unit types on offer rather than generic stock imagery.

03

Infrastructure, itemised

Where an estate's facility list is published — roads, security, drainage, water, power — we carry that exact list through to its property page.

04

Security

Several estates specify 24-hour security, perimeter fencing or gated access as part of their standard offering.

05

Documentation

Where an estate is marketed as FCDA-approved or carrying a Certificate of Occupancy, that status is shown on its page — and where it isn't stated, we say so rather than assume it.

06

Professional service & support

One Gwarimpa office coordinates enquiries, inspections and after-sale support across all nine estates.

07

Investment potential

Phased pricing — visible in Dominion Villa's documented 10 August 2026 increment — means earlier buyers on a given estate typically pay less than later buyers on the same plot size.

08

Straightforward pricing

Outright and installment prices are published side by side, with promotional discounts clearly marked where they apply.
